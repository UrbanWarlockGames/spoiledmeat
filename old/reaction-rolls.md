## Reaction Roll

Roll to see who reacts first in Narrative Mode, or to see if your character can react before something happens.

Roll [Agility] or [Smarts] + the assigned Skill of the Reaction type.

### Example Triggers:
- You are about to be seen by an enemy

### Player Moves
- Strike! First [Strike! roll]: Make a Strike!. Increase Scene Tension, Opponents go first if Initiative is called (only if Tactical Mode is needed).
- Avoid Notice [Stealth]: Move 1/2 and Hide. Decrease Scene Tension.
- De-escalate [Charm]: Decrease Scene Tension, at least temporarily.
- Signal an Ally [Rally]: Give +1 to an Ally's Reaction roll.
- Duck and Cover [Acrobatics]: Move and Take Cover or Fall Prone, Increase Scene Tension, Opponents go first
- Create a Diversion [Distract]: Attempt to attract attention away from someone else. Do something loud, flashy, or dangerous.
- Negotiate [Persuade]: Decrease Scene Tension
- Interrupt: You stop, delay, or disrupt the action before it completes.
- Read the Situation [Insight]: Ask a question about a Target within Range.
- Power Up [Power roll]: Use an Ability.
- Brace [Endure]: Raise a Shield. This lasts until the end of your next turn.
